Our Story & Philosophy

A Symphony of Flour, Sugar & Pure Passion

Sweet Crumbs Bakery was born from a simple belief: life's sweetest memories deserve extraordinary flavors. Founded in 2018 by pastry chef Eléonore after training in Paris, our boutique kitchen blends classical European baking traditions with modern romantic aesthetics.

Every sourdough loaf rests for 24 hours. Every croissant is laminated with silky grass-fed butter. And every custom celebration cake is piped and decorated by hand with meticulous care. No premade mixes, no artificial preservatives — just pure, honest craft.
